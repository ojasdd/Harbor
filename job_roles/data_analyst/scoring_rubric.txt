Interview Evaluation Rubric:

Technical Accuracy:
10 - Fully correct with depth
7-9 - Mostly correct, minor omissions
4-6 - Partial understanding
1-3 - Major inaccuracies

Clarity:
10 - Clear and concise
7-9 - Mostly understandable
4-6 - Some ambiguity
1-3 - Poor communication

Completeness:
10 - Covers all major points
7-9 - Covers most points
4-6 - Missing important details
1-3 - Very incomplete

Communication:
10 - Professional and structured
7-9 - Understandable
4-6 - Weak structure
1-3 - Difficult to follow
